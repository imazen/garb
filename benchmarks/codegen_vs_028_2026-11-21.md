## 1. Summary of AVX2 Codegen Improvements (`src/bytes/avx2.rs`)

1. **Eliminated in-loop bounds checks & unlocked 4× SIMD unrolling (`gray_alpha_to_4bpp_row_v3`, `gray_to_4bpp_row_v3`, `copy_*_row_v3`):**
   - Previously, `while is + 16 <= slen && id + 32 <= dlen` with separate `is`/`id` counters kept a `cmpq; ja` bounds check inside every 8-pixel iteration and prevented LLVM from unrolling `gray_alpha_to_rgba` (`77.9 GiB/s` in L2).
   - Slicing to `npx = (src.len() / 2).min(dst.len() / 4)` and iterating `.as_chunks::<16>()` / `.as_chunks_mut::<32>()` removes the in-loop bounds branch, eliminates `slice_index_fail` landing pads, and unlocks 4× AVX2 unrolling (**`77.9 -> 157 GiB/s`, `2.02×` faster in L2; `78.7 -> 88.4 GiB/s`, `-11.0%` faster at `2 MiB`**).
2. **Eliminated dead `<8`-pixel vector tail bloat via counter-indexed `0..rem_px.len()` tails (`swap_br_row_v3`, `fill_alpha_row_v3`, `rotate_*_row_v3`, `reverse_4bpp_row_v3`, `rgb_to_argb_row_v3`):**
   - Using `0..rem_px.len()` (and packed `u32` stores in `rgb_to_argb_row_v3` / `rgb_to_abgr_row_v3`) exposes the exact `< 8` remainder range to LLVM's `ScalarEvolution`, cutting full-kernel LTO instruction counts by `-16` to `-114` instructions on `x86-64-v3`/`x86-64-v4` (compiling `<8`-pixel tails to a single branchless `vpmaskmovd` on AVX2 or `vmovdqu32 {%k1}` on AVX-512).
